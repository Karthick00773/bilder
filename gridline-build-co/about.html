About Gridline Build Co.

Our story

A reliable contractor for the complete construction process

Gridline Build Co. is a full-service construction contractor. When you hand us a house construction contract, we manage the entire process ourselves — labour arrangement, centering and shuttering work, structural construction, materials coordination, day-to-day site supervision, and final completion.

That means one accountable team on your site from the day the first trench is dug to the day you're handed the keys, rather than a chain of disconnected sub-contractors. We plan the schedule, arrange skilled labour, execute the civil and structural work, coordinate plumbing and electrical trades, and see the finishing through — so the building is genuinely ready to use when we hand it over.

Our process

How we run your project

A fixed sequence we follow on every contract, from the first site visit to the day you move in.

PH–01

Survey & Estimate

Site visit, measurement and a detailed cost estimate.

PH–02

Contract & Planning

Scope, schedule and materials plan agreed and signed off.

PH–03

Labour & Centering

Crew mobilised; centering and shuttering for foundation.

PH–04

Structural Work

Concrete, mason work and civil construction to plinth & roof.

PH–05

MEP Coordination

Plumbing and electrical work coordinated with our supervisors.

PH–06

Finishing & Handover

Painting, finishing, final inspection and key handover.
